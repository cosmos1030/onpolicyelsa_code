## What this box learned about 8B PGD training

**It needs two GPUs, and not for the reason the launcher comments suggest.**
Three single-GPU attempts died at the same place:

* `--gmp_kl_chunk_size` was never passed by either 8B launcher, so it defaulted
  to 0 and the full-vocab KD log-softmax ran in one piece. Fixed (commit
  `b5a8313`); both now pass `${KL_CHUNK_SIZE:-256}`.
* With that fixed the OOM moves to the NTP cross-entropy inside HF's
  `ForCausalLMLoss`, which no flag here chunks. Chunk sizes down to 64 did not
  help.
* `main.py`'s own flag help is the giveaway: those tensors are "~5GB each in
  fp32 **regardless of GPU count (not FSDP-sharded)**". Adding a GPU does not
  shrink them — what FSDP buys is sharding params, grads and optimizer state,
  which is where the headroom comes from.

8B ALPS+SFT *does* fit on one card, because `gmp_fixed_mask=true` drops the
dense-model tracking and the PGD candidate machinery entirely.

`b200_scripts/alps_sft_ntpkd_opkd_qwen3_8b_fsdp2gpu.sh` is new: a 2-GPU fork,
equivalent by construction (1 GPU = batch 1 x accum 8 x ws 1; 2 GPU = 1 x 4 x 2;
both 8 sequences/step). Measured 6.5 step/min single-GPU vs 9.2 on two, which is
what made two 8B trainings fit in one night.

  in `main.py`, in `lighteval_bench.run`'s signature, and hardcoded in five
